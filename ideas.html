Ttukseom Hangang Park · Ttukseom Resort Stn (Line 7) · ~20:30 · free

The creator's own comment lists the autumn dates: 12 Sep, 9 Oct and 31 Oct — Marvel themed. Cultural performances before and after, farmers' market and food discounts on site. Watch from anywhere along the bank.

We arrive back in Seoul that afternoon and check into the hanok at 16:00. The evening was pencilled in as low-key — this is the one thing worth breaking that for. Getting home will be the crush. source

Seoul Forest garden route + Seoul My Soul Loungecloses 27 Oct
Seoul Forest, Seongdong-gu · Seoul Forest / Ttukseom Stn · 10:30–21:00 · free

A signposted route through the Seoul International Garden Show: the K-Heritage Garden (traditional walls, a numaru pavilion, terraced flower beds), Sitting in Seoul — wildfire-damaged trees remade into benches — and Green Pulse, a peatland-and-meadow garden about the carbon cycle. It ends at the Seoul My Soul Lounge on the Central Lawn: Seoul goods, a food zone and brand shops. Posted by the official city account.

The only new find with an expiry date inside our window — the lounge runs to Mon 27 Oct, the morning we take the KTX south. Realistically Fri 23 afternoon (a big free park is good jet-lag terrain) or Mon 26 before the Everland shuttle. source

Skyline Luge BusanFri 30 Oct?
San 60-1, Sirang-ri, Gijang-eup, Gijang-gun · ~10:00–18:00 · 30–40 min NE of Haeundae

Gravity karts down a hillside track, plus a zipline. The rule is 110cm and age 6+ to ride solo — at 132cm and 8 she drives her own. An accompanying adult needs to be 150cm+ if you'd rather double up. Two separate saves point here, including one from a family who spent 13 nights in Busan.

Now competing with Club D Oasis for the empty Friday. The luge is the bigger thrill; the water park is the easier day. Check the operator's closure notice the day before — it wasn't running on the day we looked. source 1 · source 2

Club D Oasis — spa & water parkFri 30 Oct
30 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan

All-use ticket ₩38,000 for 10 hours — most Busan spas cap you at four. Swim cap and life vest compulsory in the water park; vests rent for ₩8,000. The creator is explicit that it isn't a white-knuckle slide park, which is exactly what we want.

Best new find for Busan. Fri 30 Oct is our deliberately-empty day and it's ten minutes from the Grand Josun. source

Travel tips

Incheon Smart Passdo this now

Pre-register face and passport and clear departure immigration in ~15 minutes instead of 60–90. The 2 Nov morning is tight — hanok at 09:00, AREX, 13:20 VS209. source

VAT refund at ICNfree money

Carry your passport when shopping; spend ≥₩15,000 at tax-free-certified shops and ask for the VAT receipt at the till. At ICN, scan receipts at the kiosks on the departures level before checking bags, then collect cash airside. Keep luxury buys unboxed for customs. Budget ~15 extra minutes. source

What not to packcustoms

Not Korea-specific, but the principle holds: cold medicine above certain pseudoephedrine thresholds, pepper spray, vapes and fresh food all change legal status the moment they cross a border. Worth a glance at any prescription or strong cold remedy before it goes in the case. source

Seoul Station city check-innot for VS

Full check-in, bag drop and immigration at Seoul Station B2 before boarding AREX — but Virgin Atlantic isn't a participating airline, and our practical notes already record this. Ignore unless VS is added. source

Climate Card — tourists unaffecteddo the maths

The 30-day Climate Card ends in August 2026, but that change is for residents. The short-term tourist cards — 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 day, unlimited Seoul subway and bus — carry on unchanged. K-Pass and Modu Card are the resident alternatives and don't concern us. Posted by @experiencekorea_naver citing the Seoul Metropolitan Government.

Worth a sum for the four-day front leg: the card pays off at roughly four or five journeys a day, which we'll hit on the palace and theme-park days but not on the DMZ day. Check whether a child version exists before buying three — if not, hers stays on the registered T-money. source
