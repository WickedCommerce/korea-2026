Club D Oasis — spa & water parkFri 30 Oct
30 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan

All-use ticket ₩38,000 for 10 hours — most Busan spas cap you at four. Swim cap and life vest compulsory in the water park; vests rent for ₩8,000. The creator is explicit that it isn't a white-knuckle slide park, which is exactly what we want.

Best new find for Busan. Fri 30 Oct is our deliberately-empty day and it's ten minutes from the Grand Josun. source

Eunpyeong Hanok Villagevs Bukchon
Base of Bukhansan · 10-min bus from Yeonsinnae Stn (Line 3)

Saved twice, independently, both saying the same thing: Bukchon is packed, steep, and the residents have had enough. Eunpyeong is quiet and spacious, with modern hanoks framed against the mountain. Café Irin Iljan (1in 1jan) has the view.

Challenges Sat 24 afternoon. The cost is ~40 minutes each way from Insadong, on the day we're already doing palaces and hanbok. source 1 · source 2

Travel tips

Incheon Smart Passdo this now

Pre-register face and passport and clear departure immigration in ~15 minutes instead of 60–90. The 2 Nov morning is tight — hanok at 09:00, AREX, 13:20 VS209. source

VAT refund at ICNfree money

Carry your passport when shopping; spend ≥₩15,000 at tax-free-certified shops and ask for the VAT receipt at the till. At ICN, scan receipts at the kiosks on the departures level before checking bags, then collect cash airside. Keep luxury buys unboxed for customs. Budget ~15 extra minutes. source

Seoul Station city check-innot for VS

Full check-in, bag drop and immigration at Seoul Station B2 before boarding AREX — but Virgin Atlantic isn't a participating airline, and our practical notes already record this. Ignore unless VS is added. source

Seoul → Busanmatches plan

Stay near Seoul Station, book KTX on the SmartEx app, travel light and leave the big case behind. ENA Suite is five minutes from Seoul Station and the storage plan is already in the itinerary. source
