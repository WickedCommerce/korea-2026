Never sit in the pink priority seats** on the subway, even when empty — they are for pregnant women. **3. Shoes off** in homes and some restaurants; look for the raised floor. **4. Dining:** don't lift your rice bowl, use the spoon for rice, never stand chopsticks upright in rice. **5. Subways and buses are near-silent** — text rather than call. **6.
