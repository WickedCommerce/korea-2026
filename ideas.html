VAT / tax refund at ICNstrong candidate

VAT / tax refund at ICN. Carry your passport when shopping, spend ≥₩15,000 at tax-free-certified shops, ask for the VAT receipt at the till. At ICN, scan receipts at the kiosks on the departures level before checking bags, then collect cash airside. Keep luxury buys unboxed for customs. — 🟡 Free money on the K-beauty haul; adds ~15 min to the airport plan.

Climate Card change — tourists are NOT affected27 Oct

Climate Card change — tourists are NOT affected. The 30-day Climate Card ends in August 2026 and is being replaced for residents; the short-term tourist Climate Cards (1/2/3/5/7-day, unlimited Seoul subway + bus) continue unchanged. K-Pass and Modu Card are the resident alternatives and are irrelevant to us. Posted by @experiencekorea_naver, sourcing Seoul Metropolitan Government. — 🟡 Worth a sum. We have 4 Seoul days at the front (23–27 Oct) and 2 at the back. A 5-day card for the first leg vs topping up T-money: the card pays off at roughly 4–5 journeys a day, which we'll hit on the palace and theme-park days but not on the DMZ day. ⚠️ Note the daughter's fare — check whether the tourist Climate Card is sold as a child version before buying three; if not, hers stays on the registered T-money.
