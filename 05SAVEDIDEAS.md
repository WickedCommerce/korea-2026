## 4. Travel tips

### Airport & flying home — read before 2 Nov

- **Incheon Smart Pass / skip the immigration queue.** Pre-register face and passport; one creator cleared departure in 15 minutes instead of 60–90. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DZCoHj4B_kI) — 🟡 **Do this.** The 2 Nov morning is tight (hanok 09:00 → AREX → 13:20 VS209).
- **VAT / tax refund at ICN.** Carry your passport when shopping, spend ≥₩15,000 at tax-free-certified shops, ask for the VAT receipt at the till. At ICN, scan receipts at the kiosks on the **departures level before checking bags**, then collect cash airside. Keep luxury buys unboxed for customs. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DbnPdOqiio6) — 🟡 Free money on the K-beauty haul; adds ~15 min to the airport plan.
- **Seoul Station City Airport Terminal (B2)** — full check-in, bag drop **and immigration** before boarding AREX. ⚠️ **Conflicts with 03PRACTICALNOTES:** Virgin Atlantic is not a participating airline and we've already recorded "no Seoul Station in-city check-in". Treat this post as not applicable unless VS is added. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DbIgjeSkqIB)

### Getting around

- **Seoul → Busan:** stay near Seoul Station, book KTX on the **SmartEx** app, travel light and leave the big case behind. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DQLAnPfAX4f) — 🟢 ENA Suite is 5 min from Seoul Station; the case-storage plan already matches.
- **Climate Card change — tourists are NOT affected.** The 30-day Climate Card ends in August 2026 and is being replaced for residents; the **short-term tourist Climate Cards (1/2/3/5/7-day, unlimited Seoul subway + bus) continue unchanged**. K-Pass and Modu Card are the resident alternatives and are irrelevant to us. Posted by @experiencekorea_naver, sourcing Seoul Metropolitan Government. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DbaUZu3E8dJ) — 🟡 **Worth a sum.** We have 4 Seoul days at the front (23–27 Oct) and 2 at the back. A **5-day card** for the first leg vs topping up T-money: the card pays off at roughly 4–5 journeys a day, which we'll hit on the palace and theme-park days but not on the DMZ day. ⚠️ Note the daughter's fare — check whether the tourist Climate Card is sold as a child version before buying three; if not, hers stays on the registered T-money.
- **eSIM instead of a pocket wifi.** Generic "get an eSIM before you fly" reel — no specific provider named in the caption; the link is behind a comment-gate. [post](https://instagram.com/p/Db85j8apn5O) — ⚪ Principle is sound (Airalo/Klook Korea eSIMs are cheap), but note the **Discover Seoul Pass already bundles an eSIM** — don't buy twice.
- **Choose accommodation by subway proximity on Naver Maps** — before anything else. Neighbourhood read: Myeongdong (first-timers) · Seongsu (trendy) · Gangnam (luxury + beauty) · Yeouido (calm) · Hongdae (nightlife). [post](https://instagram.com/p/DaaYFEHDPt6) — 🟢 All three of our bases are subway-adjacent.

- **CatchTable — join restaurant queues from your phone.** Korea's remote-waitlist app: register on the tablet at the door or remotely, then walk the neighbourhood instead of standing in line. Named independently by a Seoul eats round-up and a Mullae restaurant post. [post 1](https://instagram.com/p/DV4jqFfkwA2) · [post 2](https://instagram.com/p/DariOUxRseo) — 🟡 **Install this before we fly.** Every high-demand spot in this catalogue — Wooyeonhwa, the Ikseon *yajang* places, Daeseung — is a queueing venue, and an hour in a queue with an 8-year-old is an hour lost.
- **LG U+ booth at ICN — SIM/eSIM plus the CHECK iN SEOUL card in one stop.** Unlimited data and a **Korean phone number**, which matters because restaurant reservations and taxi apps often demand one. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DYBWWWVPGay) — 🟡 **The Korean number is the part we have no plan for.** Weigh against the eSIM-before-you-fly route and the Discover Seoul Pass bundle — don't buy the same thing three times.

[post](https://instagram.com/p/DZfIzXSTHHw) — ⚪ Low-stakes, but a free T-money card is real money ×3, and K-beauty sampling is itself a **Sun 1 Nov** Myeongdong activity.

- **Six cultural mistakes tourists make** — worth reading aloud on the plane. **1. Never tip** (the menu price is final; money left on a table just confuses people). **2. Never sit in the pink priority seats** on the subway, even when empty — they are for pregnant women. **3. Shoes off** in homes and some restaurants; look for the raised floor. **4. Dining:** don't lift your rice bowl, use the spoon for rice, never stand chopsticks upright in rice. **5. Subways and buses are near-silent** — text rather than call. **6. Toilet paper** — bin it where a bin is provided, only flush in modern buildings, check the sign. **7. No eating or drinking on the subway or bus** — @southkorea.explores adds this one, and says security staff can put you off. **8. Avoid the subway at peak hours** if you can help it. [post](https://instagram.com/p/DcEZNMhElXR) · [post 2](https://instagram.com/p/DchcPU1ghME) — 🟡 *Points 2, 4, 5 and 7 are the ones an 8-year-old can actually get wrong. Five minutes on the plane.* Point 8 has a practical edge for us: **no packed-carriage transfers with a case** — the 23 Oct arrival and the 27 Oct Seoul→Busan KTX morning both want an off-peak slot.
- **Korea's everyday tech, as a spotting game.** From a 10-item round-up: robots that slide under cars, lift the wheels and park them · delivery robots that call the lift themselves and ride up to your door · in-ground LED traffic lights at crossings, for people staring at phones · Songdo's underground vacuum waste pipes · RFID food-waste bins that weigh and bill each household (Korea recycled 96.8% of its food waste in 2023).
